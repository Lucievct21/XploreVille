<strong>Stop 6 - Piazza Santa Maria Liberatrice</strong>
We’ve now arrived at Piazza Santa Maria Liberatrice, a central spot for community life in Testaccio.
Its name comes from the parish church facing the square, built in the early 20th century as a tribute to an old church in the Roman Forum.
The square is lively: there’s a fountain, a small garden, a playground…
And it’s also where the neighborhood festival takes place every year - a much-loved event eagerly awaited by the locals.
