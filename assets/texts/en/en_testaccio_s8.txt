<strong>Stop 8 - Piazza Testaccio</strong>
Here we are at Piazza Testaccio, the final stop on our walk and the true beating heart of the neighborhood.
For a long time, this square hosted the market before it moved to Via Galvani. Since then, it’s been redesigned, but it hasn’t lost any of its energy.
At any time of the day or night, you’ll find real Roman life here: elderly folks chatting early in the morning, kids playing ball in the afternoon, and young people gathering in the evening around a beer, sitting on the edge of the fountain.
That fountain, by the way, is called the Fountain of the Amphora, a tribute to Mount Testaccio and its millions of amphora shards that gave the neighborhood its name.
And so, the circle is complete.
